✅ How Option 1 Works:

  • Fake line numbers div sits beside textarea with overflow-y: scroll
  • Counts \n characters to determine line count
  • Updates on every keystroke
  • Scroll position syncs automatically via JavaScript listener
  • Can be toggled on/off with a button
  • Works on all browsers and devices

⚠️ Cons of Option 1:

Option 1 is the clear winner for our use case! It's easy to implement, works great with textareas, requires no refactoring, and provides a professional IDE-like experience. The scroll syncing is smooth, and toggling works perfectly on all devices.
